Portfolio

Below are some projects on which I have worked, either independently or as part of a team. The projects range from metadata crosswalks to digital creations.

Fredericton Polio Clinic

Using archival sources (mainly images) from the Provincial Archives of New Brunswick, developed a wordpress website on the history of poliomyelitis in New Brunswick. Skills : Archival research, web development, sitemaps, wireframes

Metadata crosswalks and analysis

Participated as a subject matter expert and produced metadata schema and crosswalks for various information sources encoded in XML schema and MARC21. Skills : MARC21, XML, metadata schema, Python

Digital Exhibit : Legal information

Co-led, a team developing an online exhibit about the history of law in Quebec and the works that have shaped the practice of law, including discussions with historians and legal scholars.
Skills : Wordpress, historical research, metadata, project management

Data mining to predict autism diagnosis in children

Used RapidMiner to create a classification model which predicts whether a child should be referred to a specialist for formal autism spectrum disorder diagnosis.
Skills : RapidMiner, Data mining, classification analysis
