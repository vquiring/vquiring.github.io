Vanessa Quiring

An information science leader and professional passionate about the intersection of technology and information. I have over ten years of experience in libraries. With experience in law libraries, I apply data mining and data-driven decision making to information science and information architecture projects. I have spent the last several years honing my education and skills in my field while continuing to push for innovative solutions to legal information problems. I now hold ICAgile certification. A proponent of access to information and knowledge, I have worked for years to implement solutions that render legal information findable and usable.
