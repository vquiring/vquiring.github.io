Welcome to Fredericton!

Nestled along the shores of the Wolastoq River (previously known as the St. John River) in central New Brunswick lies the capital city of Fredericton. First established here were the Wolastoqi, followed by the Acadians, New England Planters and Loyalists in the eighteenth and early nineteenth centruies.

As the capital region of the only bilingual province in Canada, Fredricton is rich in heritage and is known for its autumnal beauty, rich and diverse food scene and as a home for families.
